Composition:

In the lower-left foreground, a sloping shoulder of weathered grey rock, low coastal shrubs and broad, soft tufts of grass partly blocks the sea. A short CONTINUOUS hiking path of 6 to 8 irregular, broad, shallow natural stone steps climbs diagonally around that shoulder toward a safe, wide earthen-and-stone platform in the lower-middle. Every tread connects plausibly to the next; feet can actually rest flat on the steps; no isolated floating stones, cliff-edge stepping challenge, stairs running into the sea or bright racetrack border.
The single blue-clothed chibi hiker is near the last two steps, stepping uphill onto one clear stone tread, facing mostly right with a slight three-quarter rear angle toward the view. Show weight over the planted boot and a modest raised knee, no exaggerated lunge or giant head redesign. Standing figure height about 11 to 12 percent of total image height. Include a low, broad natural resting stone beside the platform, presently empty, which could later support a seated pose.
The upper and right portions open into a clear high-view panorama: curved white-sand coastlines separated by green headlands, turquoise shallows leading to deeper blue sea, layered green hills and the recognizable pointed profile of Sharp Peak in the distance. It is a Hong Kong coastal hill, not an Alpine or snowy peak. Keep the sea, coves and peak readable rather than obscured by foreground plants or clouds. The eye follows the steps, the hiker, then the bay and Sharp Peak.
Keep the important steps, platform, hiker and distant pointed mountain within a generous central safe composition so a later portrait-camera view can be designed. No split panels, storyboards, duplicate characters, collage, framing mat, badge or interface elements.

Lighting and texture:
A clear warm-season morning with soft sunlight from the upper left, coherent gentle contact shadows on the stone treads and boots, natural coastal olive/sage greens, weathered pale-grey stone, creamy white sand and luminous restrained blue-green water. Fine-grain watercolor paper, delicate pigment blooms, carefully painted coastal grasses rather than needle-like grass spikes. Distant hills softer and cooler; near stone and plant details crisp enough for interactive footholds. Sky is a modest part of the picture with a few light clouds, never a cloud sea concealing the mountains.
