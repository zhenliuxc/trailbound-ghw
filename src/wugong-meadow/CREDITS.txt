Wugong elevation mesh, a slope / road / camp coverage attribute, shorter blade
dimensions, a muted palette and a material adapter for fog and pigment variation.

The authored mesh, backpack, skeleton, Walk and Idle_Neutral animations are used.
Idle_Neutral is renamed Idle. Other clips and horizontal root motion are removed.
Materials are recoloured to sage green, grey and brown, with matte surfaces.
Three.js AnimationMixer blends the two clips. A two-bone terrain adapter keeps
the knees forward, supports independent shoe controls, and samples sole contact.
No separate authored uphill / downhill / sitting animation is included.
The asset is repacked with glTF Transform 4.5.1 (MIT build tools).
https://gltf-transform.dev/
Source hashes and transformations: art/wugong-hiker/provenance-v2.json in the repository.
No image texture is downloaded for the character.

Three seeded Ash Medium silhouettes with two detail levels, instanced in groves.
The bundled ash leaf alpha texture is resized to 256px and neutralised while
retaining the leaf silhouettes and vein shading. No bark textures are bundled.
Branch generation uses the original package source. Texture loading, palette,
grove distribution, instancing and the matching leaf / shadow wind shader are
Trailbound adapters. These are scenic broadleaf trees, not surveyed local species.
Source hashes and changes: art/wugong-trees/provenance-v1.json in the repository.

Watercolor chapters and notebook reuse the original PixiJS game art and saved
field-journal key. A finished interaction reveals art; pressing collect stores it.
